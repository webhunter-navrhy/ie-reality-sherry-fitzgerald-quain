Sherry FitzGerald Quain are delighted to bring to the market this beautiful 4 Bedroom detached home (c.223sq.m) in a quiet residential cul-de-sac in close proximity to Athlone town centre and all its amenities. Presented in 'impeccable order' both inside and out, this fine home offers generous accommodation and is 'ready and waiting' to be occupied by its new owners.

10 Cluain Ri represents an outstanding opportunity for the discerning purchaser who is looking for a high-standard suburban home. Located just 7 km from Athlone and strolling distance to Hodson Bay Hotel & Leisure Centre, Athlone Golf Club and Lough Ree. This fine home on a cul de sac permeates a tranquil elegance which features three fine reception areas, four bedrooms, three baths and detached large garage. The living-dining areas afford wonderful views of the landscaped gardens surrounding the property. The property offers 2400 sq. ft. (223 sq. m) approximately and stands on a c. 0.54 acre elevated site. There is an extensive tarmac driveway with ample off street car parking, to the rear there is a large slabbed patio with raised planting and mature gardens. Many extras and features are included in this fine property such as wooden floors, two solid fuel stoves, high spec kitchen with granite tops, and superb fully tiled bathrooms. The property is in turnkey condition.
